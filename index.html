THE 10-HOUR ENGAGEMENT

A fixed-scope diagnostic for founders and investors.

I run 10-hour retention, growth, and diligence diagnostics for consumer software and DTC brands — no fee for the first pass, no commercial teaser attached. You get evidence, a prioritized diagnosis, and a real recommendation. Not a pitch deck.

  • ✓ Eight-workstream diagnosis — acquisition quality, early behavior, lifecycle, transactional, account & subscription, save motions, data, involuntary churn.
  • ✓ Artifacts, not chat. Raw pulls in the project, one durable analysis per swimlane, a hypothesis index with evidence.
  • ✓ Independent attribution guardrail. Platform self-attribution doesn't count on its own; findings get corroborated.
  • ✓ Evidence can reject the story. Priors get overturned when the data says so — that's the whole point.
